Learn the numbers

Safe to spend

Money Dex estimates may be available after protected funds and near-term essentials. This is not a bank balance or a guarantee; check all upcoming bills and your accounts before spending.

Tracked cash

Your starting balance, plus recorded income, minus recorded expenses. Reconcile regularly against your actual accounts.

Already protected

Money assigned within Dex to bills, taxes, necessities, optional savings, or discretionary spending. Dex does not move real money.

Unassigned cash

Money not yet assigned to a category. Upcoming obligations may still need it, so it's not necessarily spendable.

Upcoming bill gap

Money needed for bills inside your current planning window that is not yet protected.

Essential spending gap

Estimated necessary spending in the planning window minus money already protected for essentials.

Uncovered essentials

The amount of near-term bills or necessities still unfunded after considering unassigned cash. A funding gap is a number to address, not a personal failure.

Guided adjustments

Dex recommends changes to flexible allocations; you approve or ignore them. Approved adjustments affect only this app's plan.

About this beta

Pay modes are available, but automatic paycheck forecasting is not yet implemented. Bills roll forward only when you mark them paid in the Bills tab. Expenses entered separately do not automatically match bills. Tax reserves are estimates. This app is not a substitute for checking actual balances before paying bills or making financial decisions.
